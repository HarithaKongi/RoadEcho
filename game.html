THE CORE IDEA

Run 1 records your steering path.

After the first phase, your old route becomes a glowing Echo car.

The Echo repeats your past decisions, turning your own driving history into an opponent.

Later, memory gates and shards appear. Survive, collect shards and create a better route for your next run.

CONTROLS

Keyboard: ← → or A D. Space = phase shift. P = pause. M = audio.

Mobile: use the left/right buttons or drag on the road.
